Policies

What to know before you book

No small print: this is everything that applies to any booking.

Check-in and check-out
Check-in from 4 to 7 pm. Check-out before 11 am. Early and late check-in are subject to availability; let us know ahead and we will arrange it.
Adults only
The house welcomes guests aged 21 and over only. It is not a hotel for children or teenagers, and we would rather say so before you book.
Keypad entry
The main door opens with a code we send the day before. If your flight lands late, write to us and we will coordinate your arrival.
Minimum stay
Two nights in regular season. Three nights in high season, holiday weekends and over New Year.
Cancellation
Free until 7 days before arrival. After that, the first night is retained.
Payment
American Express, Visa, Mastercard and Diners Club, as well as bank transfer. Rates are in US dollars and charged at the day’s exchange rate.
Security deposit
A refundable deposit is requested before arrival and released on check-out if there is no damage. (Amount to be confirmed with the house.)
Breakfast
Included in the rate, for two people per suite, from 8 to 11 am.
Pool
Open from 8 am to midnight, with loungers, umbrellas and cabanas at no charge.
Pets
We do not accept pets or service animals, for hygiene and for the rest of our other guests.
Smoking
The whole property is smoke-free, inside the suites and in the shared areas.
Accessibility
The house has three floors and no lift. Luna is the only suite reached without stairs.
Quiet hours
From 10 pm we keep noise down in the shared areas. This is a house, not a party venue.
The town at night
Sayulita has nightlife, and in high season the music carries into the early hours. The interior suites face the garden and are the most sheltered; every room has earplugs and a white-noise machine. If you are a light sleeper, tell us when booking and we will put you in the quietest room available.
Whole house
Whole-house bookings are quoted separately and have their own deposit and cancellation terms.

Any questions?

Write to us before booking. We answer the same day.
