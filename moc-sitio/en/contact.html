Getting here

From Puerto Vallarta airport
About 45 minutes by car. There are authorised taxis at the terminal, and private transfers we can arrange for you.
By car
Take federal highway 200 north. Sayulita has a single entrance; once in the centre, Marlin crosses half a block from the plaza.
Parking
The house has two covered spaces and two on the street. Sayulita’s streets are narrow, so let us know your arrival time.
Around town
Everything is walkable. For San Pancho or Punta Mita, shared vans leave from the entrance of town.
