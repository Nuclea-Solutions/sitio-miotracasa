Privacy notice

Data controller
Mi Otra Casa, at Calle Marlin 18, Sayulita, Nayarit, Mexico, is responsible for the processing of your personal data.
What we collect
Name, email address, phone number, stay dates and whatever you choose to tell us in the form message.
Why we use it
To answer your booking request, confirm availability, provide the accommodation and, if you agree to it, send you offers from the house.
Who we share it with
We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except the providers needed to run the booking, who are bound to protect it.

You may access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent, by writing to contact@miotracasa.mx.
